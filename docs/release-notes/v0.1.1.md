**Install**
- **Windows x64:** `Emdi-Setup-0.1.1.exe`. Emdi 0.1.0 updates itself to this version.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Emdi-0.1.1-mac-arm64.dmg` (ad-hoc signed: right-click › Open the first time).
- **Linux x64:** `Emdi-0.1.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage`.

**Right-to-left text reads right-to-left**
- Persian, Arabic and Hebrew paragraphs, headings, list items and table cells take their direction from the language most of their letters are in, so a Persian sentence that starts with an English word (`React یک کتابخانه است`) stays right-to-left. An English paragraph in a Persian document stays left-to-right.
- Lists put their bullets and numbers on the right, tables run their columns right-to-left, and quotes and alerts move their border to the right.
- A document that opens in Persian lays out right-to-left: "On this page" moves to the left and the Outline indents from the right.
- Inline code, keyboard keys and math keep their own order inside right-to-left text; numbered headings show `1.2` whole.
- Persian headings keep their letters joined (no letter spacing), and emphasis shows as semi-bold instead of a fake italic.
- Headings and file names in the Outline, Explorer, Search Everywhere and Find in Files show in their own direction.
